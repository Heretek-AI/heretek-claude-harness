## Ideation Notes

*definitional — this test IS the external-data triangulation approach.* External-data triangulation is the methodology of searching external sources, finding where multiple sources agree on a problem, and synthesizing new techniques by combining existing ones. This test issue codifies the methodology itself so its effectiveness can be measured over time. The "ideation" was the methodology's: this is not a deliverable produced by the methodology — it IS the methodology's measurement instrument.

## Scope

## Measurement framework

**Definition of "effective ideation" for this approach:**
An external-data-triangulation-produced roadmap item is *effective* iff (a) it ships on time, (b) its success criteria are met without major rework, (c) it spawns ≤1 follow-up issue per shipped item, and (d) it sees ≥1 downstream consumer within 6 months of ship.

**Measurement protocol:**
1. For each roadmap item using this approach (#66, #68, #70, #73, #74, #76, #79):
   - Ship date vs scheduled date (binary: on-time / late)
   - Success-criteria pass/fail per item's DoD
   - Follow-up issues count (from issue tracker) at M+6 and M+12
   - Downstream adoption count (catalog installs, hook invocations, CI runs)
2. Per-item scorecard at M+6, M+12, M+18, M+24.
3. Cross-item analysis at M12 and M24: which sub-patterns of external-data triangulation correlate with on-time ship + success-criteria pass?
4. Methodology verdict at M24: continue, refine, or replace.

**Success thresholds:**
- ≥4 of 7 items ship on time (approximately 57%).
- ≥4 of 7 items meet success criteria without major rework.
- Average follow-up issue count per shipped item ≤1.5.
- ≥3 of 7 items see downstream adoption within 6 months.

**Data collection:**
- Issue tracker (GitHub) for ship dates and follow-up counts.
- Catalog telemetry (when available) for adoption.
- Per-item retrospective notes captured at ship time.
- Mid-cycle check-in at M6 and M12 with go/no-go on continuing the approach.

**Decision deliverable:** M24 methodology-effectiveness report (`docs/superpowers/research/2026-08-06-external-data-ideation-report.md`) with per-item scorecard, cross-item analysis, and verdict.
