## Ideation Notes

*definitional — this test IS the cross-domain transfer ideation approach.* Cross-domain transfer ideation is the methodology of borrowing techniques from unrelated fields (biology, type theory, dynamical systems) and applying them to coding agents. This test issue codifies the methodology itself so its effectiveness can be measured over time. It is the most "novel" of the four approaches and the most validation-expensive, so its measurement is critical to the spec's methodology verdict.

## Scope

- Define "effective cross-domain transfer ideation" via measurable proxies (below).
- Identify every roadmap item that uses this approach: #71, #72, #77, #78 (4 items, per spec §3).
- Track per-item: shipped-on-time, success-criteria met, follow-up issues spawned, downstream adoption.
- Roll up at M12 and M24 with: per-item scorecard + cross-item correlation analysis + methodology-effectiveness verdict.

## Out of scope

- Measuring other ideation approaches (#80, #81, #82 cover them).
- Specifying the M24 roll-up report format.
- Cross-correlating cross-domain items with the source fields they borrowed from (separate analysis if valuable).

## Measurement framework

**Definition of "effective ideation" for this approach:**
A cross-domain-transfer-ideation-produced roadmap item is *effective* iff (a) it ships on time, (b) its success criteria are met without major rework, (c) it spawns ≤1 follow-up issue per shipped item, and (d) it sees ≥1 downstream consumer within 6 months of ship. For this approach specifically, an additional criterion is: (e) the transferred technique's source field is *cited* in the item's Ideation Notes.

**Measurement protocol:**
1. For each roadmap item using this approach (#71, #72, #77, #78):
   - Ship date vs scheduled date (binary: on-time / late)
   - Success-criteria pass/fail per item's DoD
   - Follow-up issues count at M+6 and M+12
   - Downstream adoption count
   - Source-field citation present in Ideation Notes (boolean)
2. Per-item scorecard at M+6, M+12, M+18, M+24.
3. Cross-item analysis at M12 and M24: which cross-domain-transfer sub-patterns correlate with on-time ship + success-criteria pass? Did the additional novelty criterion (criterion e) predict ship success?
4. Methodology verdict at M24: continue, refine, or replace.

**Success thresholds:**
- ≥3 of 4 items ship on time (75%) — slightly higher bar than other approaches because of higher validation cost.
- ≥3 of 4 items meet success criteria without major rework.
- Average follow-up issue count per shipped item ≤1.5.
- ≥2 of 4 items see downstream adoption within 6 months.
- Criterion (e) citation rate: 100% — every item's source field is documented.

**Data collection:**
- Issue tracker for ship dates and follow-up counts.
- Catalog telemetry (when available) for adoption.
- Per-item retrospective notes captured at ship time.
- Mid-cycle check-in at M6 and M12.

**Decision deliverable:** M24 methodology-effectiveness report (`docs/superpowers/research/2026-08-06-cross-domain-ideation-report.md`) with per-item scorecard, cross-item analysis, and verdict.
